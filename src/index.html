HOW IT WORKS

How to convert a WhatsApp chat to PDF

Export the conversation from WhatsApp, open the exported TXT or ZIP in ChatToPaper, check the preview, then download the finished PDF. Use the ZIP or select the exported photos when you want images included.

  1. Export the chat. On iPhone, open Chat Info → Export Chat. On Android, use ⋮ → More → Export chat.
  2. Open the export. Choose the TXT for text only, or the ZIP / TXT + photos when media matters.
  3. Preview and download. Check dates, names and photos, choose A4 or US Letter, then save the PDF.

FAQ

WhatsApp to PDF questions

Is the WhatsApp to PDF converter free?+

Yes. ChatToPaper is free during the current launch period, with no checkout required to create and download your PDF.

How do I convert a WhatsApp chat to PDF?+

Use WhatsApp’s Export Chat feature, then open the exported TXT or ZIP here. Review the preview and download the finished PDF.

Are my chats uploaded?+

No. Your selected chat files are processed in your browser rather than uploaded to an application server.

Can the PDF include photos?+

Yes, when the WhatsApp export includes supported JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF files. Use an export with media and keep the original attachment filenames.

Does it work with iPhone and Android exports?+

Yes. ChatToPaper accepts WhatsApp chat exports from iPhone and Android as TXT or ZIP files, including supported exported photos.

Can I convert a WhatsApp group chat to PDF?+

Yes. Export the group conversation as its own chat and ChatToPaper keeps participant names, message order and timestamps from the export.

Can a small business save WhatsApp orders and customer approvals as PDF records?+

Yes. Export the relevant conversation and keep the resulting PDF with the related order, quote, invoice, work order or project file. Treat it as supporting documentation rather than a replacement for your formal accounting or contract records.

Does this recover deleted messages?+

No. ChatToPaper formats the content in the export you provide; it cannot recover messages or media missing from that export and does not certify legal evidence.

YOUR WORDS BELONG TO YOU

Privacy, simply.

ChatToPaper processes selected conversations and images inside your browser. Your chat files and photos are not uploaded to ChatToPaper. During the limited-time free launch, no payment or checkout is required.

What stays on your device

Your selected files are held in memory while the page is open. Replacing or clearing a conversation releases the app’s references to those files; closing the page ends the session. Files you originally selected and PDFs you download remain wherever you saved them.

Your choice to share

A PDF contains the messages, names, times and images you choose to include. Review it before sharing it. Other people in a conversation may have their own privacy expectations.

Analytics

The converter remains local to your browser. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect site-usage and performance information; they do not receive the contents of the WhatsApp files you select.

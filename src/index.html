A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Good to know.

Before you turn a chat
into something you can keep.

✳
Will my messages or photos be uploaded?+

No. This standalone version reads your files and builds your PDF inside your browser. It has no upload server, analytics, tracking or external requests. Use “Clear conversation” to release the imported files, or close the tab. Any PDFs you download remain on your device.

Can I turn a WhatsApp ZIP into a PDF?+

Yes. Add a ZIP containing one exported conversation and its image files. You can also select an exported TXT file and its photos together. ChattoPaper matches the image filenames in the chat to the files in your export.

What if a photo is missing?+

You’ll see a warning before downloading, and the document will keep a labeled placeholder in the original position. A photo can only be included if the export contains both a reference to it and a readable matching image file.

Are videos, voice notes and stickers included?+

This version is for messages and ordinary JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images. GIFs are saved as a still image. Videos, voice notes, documents and other unsupported attachments are listed as placeholders. Their content is not included or transcribed.

Can I print a WhatsApp conversation from an iPhone?+

Yes. Follow the iPhone export guide above, add your export, then download the finished PDF. Open the PDF in your device’s PDF viewer to print or share it. A4 and US Letter page sizes are available under Finishing touches.

Is it really a one-time 99¢ payment?+

That’s the planned offer: one conversation, one finished PDF, 99¢, with no subscription. This preview edition does not collect payments; you can try the converter and download for free.

Does this recover deleted messages or certify a record?+

No. ChattoPaper formats the text and photos in the export you provide. It cannot recover missing or deleted messages, verify authenticity, or certify a document as legal evidence. Dates and times reflect the exported chat, with no timezone conversion.

Are there file or browser limits?+

Use a current browser with JavaScript and Canvas enabled. ZIP compatibility is built into the website, including for browsers without native ZIP decompression. File-preview viewers may not run interactive controls; open the downloaded HTML in a full browser. Imports are limited to 100 MB of selected files, 250 MB after ZIP expansion, 5,000 files and 20,000 messages. Finished documents are limited to 300 pages. Very long chats are easier to convert in a smaller date range. PDFs preserve the rendered appearance, so message text is not searchable or selectable.

YOUR WORDS BELONG TO YOU

Privacy, simply.

This standalone preview processes selected conversations and images inside your browser. It does not upload your files, use analytics, store chats in browser storage, or contact a payment service.

What stays on your device

Your selected files are held in memory while the page is open. Replacing or clearing a conversation releases the app’s references to those files; closing the page ends the session. Files you originally selected and PDFs you download remain wherever you saved them.

Your choice to share

A PDF contains the messages, names, times and images you choose to include. Review it before sharing it. Other people in a conversation may have their own privacy expectations.

About this version

This privacy notice describes the offline preview edition. Hosting and payment services would need their own disclosures if added to a public version.

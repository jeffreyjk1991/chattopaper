Are photos included?+

No. ChattoPaper formats the content in the export you provide; it does not recover deleted messages or certify legal evidence.

YOUR WORDS BELONG TO YOU

Privacy, simply.

ChattoPaper processes selected conversations and images inside your browser. Your chat files and photos are not uploaded to ChattoPaper or Stripe. When you choose to pay, a separate Stripe Checkout tab receives only the payment and checkout information needed by Stripe.

What stays on your device

Your selected files are held in memory while the page is open. Replacing or clearing a conversation releases the app’s references to those files; closing the page ends the session. Files you originally selected and PDFs you download remain wherever you saved them.

Your choice to share

A PDF contains the messages, names, times and images you choose to include. Review it before sharing it. Other people in a conversation may have their own privacy expectations.

Analytics and checkout

The converter remains local to your browser. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect site-usage and performance information; they do not receive the contents of the WhatsApp files you select. Stripe handles checkout separately; ChattoPaper does not send your conversation contents or photos to Stripe.
THE SMALL PRINT, KEPT SMALL

Using ChattoPaper.

ChattoPaper formats an exported conversation into a PDF. Use only files you are entitled to process and share. Review the preview, date interpretation and missing-photo notices before relying on your document.

A formatting tool

The tool does not recover deleted messages, authenticate a chat, certify legal evidence, or guarantee that an export is complete. It preserves what it can read and identifies unsupported or missing media.

Payments

ChattoPaper is designed as a one-time 99¢ purchase per finished PDF with no subscription. Stripe Checkout is currently connected in test mode, so test checkouts do not create a real charge.
